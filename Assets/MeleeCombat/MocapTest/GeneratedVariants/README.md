# Generated weapon bandits

For a full NPC duel, open `BoStaffFullCombatArena.unity` and press Play. Ash and Rust begin on opposite sides of the baked arena NavMesh, run toward each other, and fight with the generated Bo Staff prefab. Both use the eight directional attacks in a repeating sequence, plus reactive dodge and parry. The HUD shows health, current state, attack angle, and counts of distinct angles, dodges, and parries. Ragdoll takes over on death. NPC body damage now requires a collider contact during a committed strike; the displayed hit point is the physics contact point. The same attack cannot score twice. This remains an NPC showcase rather than final VR weapon hit detection.

The `ImportedAnimations` folder contains 47 distinct motions from the Pro Melee Axe Pack and two Great Sword motions. Loose downloads with matching motion names were omitted to avoid duplicate exports. The six one-hand generated bandits use the grounded downward, horizontal, and backhand attacks across their eight directions; the 360-degree spins and combos remain available for preview but are not fired by NPC AI. HookSword mirrors the right-hand source attacks to its left-hand grip, including in the Inspector scrub preview. The six two-hand blade/axe bandits combine their existing attack with Great Sword Slash and use Great Sword Blocking for parry. Bo Staff and Pole Blade keep their polearm clips. Other imported motions, including kicks, disarms, jumps, taunts, and unarmed actions, are available in the one-hand prefab preview playlists but are not triggered by NPC AI without matching mechanics.

Open `ImportedCombatArena.unity` and press Play to watch Axe versus HookSword use the new attack states with navigation, dodge, parry, hits, and ragdoll. To scrub a clip, open a weapon's `*Preview.unity`, select its **Animated male rig**, choose a named entry in **Mocap Staff Preview → Animations**, set **Animation Index**, turn **Playing** off, and drag **Normalized Time**. Weapon meshes and grip offsets can still be adjusted on each generated prefab.

Open `GeneratedWeaponGallery.unity` and press Play, or open an individual `*Preview.unity` scene. The 14 prefabs use the project's generated weapon meshes; the shield is temporary because there is no generated shield mesh. Across the variants, their playlists expose all 40 imported male clips.

The armed bandit pass covers all 14 generated weapons, the unified Bo Staff, and the legacy combined Bo Staff. Locomotion keeps the weapon hand or hands in a forward guard, while full-body attack clips take over during strikes. Weapon tip markers are measured from mesh vertices. Generated weapons use short convex collision sections fitted to the visible mesh; the broad dropped-loot collider turns off while an NPC holds the weapon. ShieldAndSaber has a separate physical saber. `Zandea/Melee Combat/Apply armed bandit grip and contact pass` reapplies prefab calibration, and `Zandea/Melee Combat/Validate armed bandit poses and colliders` writes coverage data and preview images to `TestCaptures/ArmedBanditPass`.

The prefabs retain `StarvingBandit`, health, `NavMeshAgent`, ragdoll, and appearance components. The previously disabled `BanditRigMotion` procedural animation driver is removed. In gameplay, `BanditMocapNpcDriver` controls the Animator from NPC state and agent velocity, and reuses the mocap grip solver after animation. `PhysicalMeleeWeapon` drives the visible weapon through a constrained physical grip target and fitted colliders. In the preview scene instances only, NPC AI, navigation, and the gameplay animation driver are disabled; the scrub preview is enabled.

Select a bandit's **Animated male rig** child to choose a clip and scrub normalized time in **Mocap Staff Preview**. The runtime controller uses eight directional run clips, the weapon's combat idle and attack, and the damage clip. Two-handed variants also have a mirrored attack state for the opposite side; the NPC chooses it for four of its eight attack directions. It never uses the knockout/death animation; `ScavengerHealth` activates ragdoll immediately. Root-motion duplicate clips are kept in the scrub playlist but not used for NavMesh locomotion. The imported FREE male pack has no dedicated dodge, parry, or block clips; those belong to the full package.

For the Bo Staff, open `BoStaffDirectionalPreview.unity` and press Play. Select **Animated male rig**, then use **Bo Staff Directional Preview** → **Direction** (0–7), **Playing**, and **Normalized Time** to watch or scrub each strike. The eight states are overhead, right diagonal, right horizontal, right low, forward thrust, left low, left horizontal, and left diagonal. They are authored clip variants built from the imported two-hand swing and polearm thrust, with adjusted torso, shoulders, and grounded lower-body motion. Gameplay chooses one of these eight states for each attack; the rest of the weapons retain their previous clips. The free pack contains only one source swing and one source thrust, so several variants still share recognizable timing and should be reviewed visually before treating them as final animation assets.

The 13 non-shield generated prefabs parry when another `StarvingBandit` starts a close strike. Two-hand weapons turn their grip axis across the body; one-hand weapons raise their own weapon axis into a diagonal guard. `ShieldAndSaber` keeps its dedicated shield block. These responses use NPC attack-state cues; incoming VR weapon detection still needs separate combat logic.

Open `ShieldBlockDuel.unity` and press Play to inspect the first shield defense slice. The shield-and-saber bandit raises and turns its temporary shield toward an incoming Bo Staff attack. Its left hand remains on the shield grip through the same IK solver. This is a visual guard response to another NPC's attack state; it does not yet stop physical damage or weapon penetration.

Open any `*ParryDuel.unity` scene and press Play to inspect that weapon's parry against a Bo Staff attacker. Each of the 13 non-shield weapons has an enabled capsule collider and dynamic rigidbody coupled to its animated desired pose through `PhysicalMeleeWeapon`. Contact can displace the actual weapon; its gripping hand or hands follow that actual pose. A weapon clash during parry briefly prevents subsequent damage from the same weapon. The installed prefabs also bake a partially closed finger pose from the imported hands-closed clip. Select the **Animated male rig** child in a preview scene to scrub attack clips and adjust grip offsets in the Inspector; preview scenes keep physics disabled for deterministic scrubbing. These are shared guard poses, so individual mesh size and guard angles may still need visual tuning. VR swing prediction, sustained pressure, and broader weapon balancing still need work.

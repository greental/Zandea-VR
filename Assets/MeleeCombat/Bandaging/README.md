# Player self-bandaging

`Assets/MyAssets/prefabs/medical/BandageRoll.prefab` uses the supplied `Assets/MyAssets/mesh/tools/bandage_roll.glb` visual, an XRI grab interactable, `JunkerEquipmentItem`, and `VRInventoryItem`. The roll stays selected in the grabbing hand. Grip grabs it; trigger while the loose tip is within `WoundWrapZone.snapDistance` of an untreated wound anchors the cloth. The other hand remains free. The existing `PlayerMartialHand` checks for a selected interactable, so the holding hand cannot enter fist stance.

`PlayerWounds` is on the `VR player - JunkerWeapon` prefab beside `ScavengerPlayerHealth`. Sharp damage registers bone-relative wound records. Each wound owns its bleed rate and visual; the player's existing health receiver applies the accumulated blood loss. Treatment reduces bleeding to `postTreatmentBleedFraction` and stops the wound particles. It never restores immediate health or changes the initial hit damage. This is separate from NPC `GoreImpactSystem`, whose timed bleed remains unchanged. Player amputations are not currently supported; ordinary wraps never restore severed parts.

At runtime, `PlayerWounds` creates `WoundWrapZone` components on both forearms, upper arms, thighs and calves from the configured `VRBodyController` bones. The values in each zone inspector control radius, length, taper, radial/axial tolerance, snap distance, and surface offset. Head and chest zones are present as extension points with physical wrapping disabled for comfort. The lower-body zones follow the existing IK bones. Quest seated reach and actual controller occlusion still require headset tuning.

The roll tracks the *controller position* in the moving bone's local frame. Angular progress is a signed net sweep. Direction locks after `directionLockDegrees`; a reversal unwinds the net progress. Invalid radial/axial samples and implausible jumps resample without progress. Controller rotation by itself does not advance. Three turns generate a mesh ribbon around the bone, with a free segment up to `looseTip`. On completion the cloth remains under the bone and the selected roll is consumed. Dropping, walking away, or taking a direct hit cancels the unfinished ribbon and leaves the original roll available for another attempt.

In Editor and development builds, F6 spawns a wound at `testRegion`; F7 cycles the eight limb regions. `createTestWoundOnStart` can seed a wound on play. The development overlay reports wound count, bleed rate, snap eligibility, angle and turns. `Kenshi VR/Bandaging/Build prefab and test scene` rebuilds the roll and its test-scene placement; `Kenshi VR/Bandaging/Validate setup and geometry` checks the prefab, inventory wiring, bone frame, and three-turn mesh. The roll is placed in `MeleePOC.unity` at approximately `(0, 1.15, 0.45)`.

**Headset pass still needed:** actual grip pose and roll scale, visibility and reach for each limb, Quest 2 tracking when passing behind limbs, wound appearance against the player's skin, blood particle cost, and completion during crouch and full-body IK movement. No connected-headset interaction was run for this slice.

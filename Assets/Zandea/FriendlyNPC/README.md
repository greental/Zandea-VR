# Friendly merchant integration

`DaranMerchantBuilder` is the authoring entry point. In Unity, choose **Zandea > Friendly NPC > Build Daran test scene**. It creates a Daran prefab, a reusable counter prefab, a personal purse, a payout pouch, dialogue and vendor assets, and `DaranMerchantTest.unity` under `Assets/Scenes/FriendlyNPC/`. The voice integration also saves `Assets/Scenes/FriendlyNPC/DaranMerchantVoiceTest.unity`, copied from the user's adjusted counter layout; open that scene to try the recorded dialogue without rebuilding the stall. Stock includes an existing physical sword and bo staff, plus an existing bandage prefab. The build checks D: free space first and stops below 20 GiB.

The merchant uses `CombinedStarvingBandit`'s CC_Base skeleton, `BanditRigMotion`, health and facial rig. Its Animator has no Avatar and is disabled by the existing rig driver. `StarvingBandit.hostileToPlayer` is false for Daran and stays true for existing bandits. Damage makes Daran hostile and ends dialogue. `MerchantHandshakePose` performs a small arm reach after `BanditRigMotion`; it only runs while an offer is ready. `NPCFacialExpressionController` remains the sole owner of the expression blendshapes and jaw. Dialogue adds a bounded speech opening to that controller's existing jaw result.

Daran clears the inherited bandit `guardAuthoringMode` flag so `BanditRigMotion` restores his base pose each frame. His head offset is applied from a stored neutral rotation rather than multiplied onto the previous frame. The unstable folded-arm IK was removed; idle is relaxed standing with restrained upper-chest breathing and an occasional small glance and a brief wave when the player approaches. The trade line presents the counter, and a valid offer extends the hand. The downloaded Lowpoly Dummy clips cannot be directly applied to this CC_Base rig because it has no Humanoid Avatar and `BanditRigMotion` disables its Animator. Reach and body language still need in-headset visual approval.

## Author dialogue and voices

### Daran's recorded pack

The project owner's 18 ElevenLabs recordings from `C:\Users\green\Downloads\Audio\DaranMerchant` are imported as real MP3 files in `Assets/Audio/Dialogue/Daran/`; the double `.wav.mp3` source suffix is normalized without transcoding. The source downloads remain untouched. `DaranVoiceBank.asset` maps semantic events to takes: `greeting_001` (two), `greeting_002` (two), `trade_offer_001` (one), `sell_ack_001` (one), `insufficient_funds_001` (two), `transaction_ready_001` (two), `trade_complete` (two), `farewell_001` (two), and three market-call groups containing four takes. Each take carries its own subtitle and 30 Hz baked mouth envelope. The voice bank avoids immediately repeating a take within a group. The two greeting groups alternate between conversations; only one greeting line plays per conversation.

`FriendlyNpcInteraction` remains the dialogue runner and requests lines by node/event ID. The counter requests a sell acknowledgment once when the first player-owned item is offered, and queues the handshake line when an offer becomes valid. Trade completion is spoken only after `TryCommit` succeeds. Ambient calls occur only while idle, at configurable 45–120 second intervals; conversation interrupts them. The 3D mouth AudioSource reaches 10 m for dialogue and 16 m for market calls. It never plays two clips simultaneously. Recorded lines are favored; dialogue nodes without a matching recording still show their authored subtitle without fabricated audio. The supplied pack has no recordings for the caravan/work line, settlement line, merchant-short-of-money line, rejected-item line, or canceled-trade line.

To add another NPC, create a `NpcVoiceBank` asset with event IDs matching its dialogue nodes and any additional contextual events. Add one or more `NpcVoiceTake`s per event; each take can override subtitle wording. Assign the bank to `FriendlyNpcInteraction` and populate `ambientEventIds` for optional idle calls. Select the bank and run **Zandea > Friendly NPC > Bake selected voice bank envelopes** before shipping. **Zandea > Friendly NPC > Import Daran voice pack** is a repeatable importer for the supplied local recordings; it preserves existing imported bytes and refuses a silent overwrite. The original ElevenLabs voice ID was not supplied, so the profile records the provider but does not invent a voice ID or require a runtime API key. Confirm the project's commercial voice rights before release.

Each `DialogueDefinition` contains stable node IDs, NPC ID, text, emotion, response links, actions, optional clips and baked mouth envelopes. `VoiceProfile` chooses the provider and voice. Nodes can override the default profile. Use `DialogueAction.QuestHook` for a future contract handler. To gate a node or response, assign a condition ID and a component implementing `IDialogueConditionSource`; a missing resolver rejects gated content.

To add an NPC, create a voice profile and dialogue asset from **Assets > Create > Zandea > Friendly NPC**. Attach `FriendlyNpcInteraction` to a compatible existing humanoid, assign the dialogue, facial controller, head AudioSource, conversation contact and palm choices, then assign any vendor counter. Avoid adding another facial controller.

Open **Zandea > Friendly NPC > Generate dialogue voice**. Select the dialogue and choose one line or the whole NPC. Generated files are placed at `Assets/Audio/Dialogue/<npc ID>/<node ID>_<hash>.wav` for Kokoro or `.mp3` for ElevenLabs. The hash includes text, provider, voice and speech rate. Existing files remain on disk when a line changes; the asset points to the new file after successful generation. An existing clip can also be dragged onto a node manually. Clip import bakes a 30 Hz amplitude envelope for the jaw, then sets compressed playback. Runtime never calls a TTS provider.

Kokoro needs `kokoro` in a local Python environment plus its model download. Set the window's Python executable to that environment. The exporter is `Tools/Voice/kokoro_generate.py`. The default `am_michael` voice is listed in the upstream voice catalog; listen to a sample before final voice approval. The [Kokoro model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) states Apache 2.0 for its weights. Check the selected voice and dependencies before distribution, and retain any required notices. No model weights are bundled here.

ElevenLabs uses `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` from the developer's environment. The key is never put in Unity assets or builds. Set `providerId` to `elevenlabs` and `providerVoiceId` to the chosen voice ID. Confirm the account's current commercial terms and voice rights before using generated clips in a release.

## Physical interaction

The merchant turns toward a nearby player, gives a short greeting cue, and beckons without forcing a conversation. Grip near the visible conversation contact, or grip Daran's hand, to begin. A raised free controller hand shows up to four responses by the palm. The opposite controller's thumbstick cycles choices and its primary button confirms; the choice colliders also accept direct interaction. Lowering the palm hides the panel. Input reservation suppresses the choosing hand's grab and martial stance, and pauses movement/turn providers while the panel is visible. Previous input states are restored when it hides. Controller axis and palm orientation require headset tuning.

Merchant stock carries `TradeItem` with an existing `NpcItemDefinition`, instance ID and legal owner ID. Only release into the Buy or Sell area registers an offer. Grabbing removes it. The counter verifies identity, owner, position and current quote again at handshake. Item price labels show a red minus for purchases and a green plus for sales. `NetCats = sale proceeds − purchase costs`.

The red Buy and green Sell pads have open holding boxes extending 0.6 m above them. A valid item released by an XR grab is detected in the matching box and frozen with its pivot clamped inside the volume, so long pieces such as the bo staff cannot roll off. Stock merely sitting on a shelf is not automatically offered. Physical capture works before conversation starts; a price only appears during an active trade. Grabbing restores its previous Rigidbody settings and removes its quote. A cancelled offer keeps its goods suspended for safe retrieval, but no price or payment remains active; release the item again after reopening trade to offer it anew. The payment spot is separate and the purse is never merchandise. The purse and payout pouch use flat BoxColliders for stable counter placement; the raised net label shows the exact amount due and whether the purse has enough cats.

`BackpackUtilityAttachments` adds `CoinPurseAnchor` and a reserved `MapAnchor` outside the storage volume. The purse is one grabbable `CurrencyPouch` with one balance. The payment spot accepts that personal purse but never prices it as merchandise. A completed debit waits until it is released, then remounts it. A merchant payout creates a separate pouch on the counter; hold the personal purse near that pouch and activate it to merge exactly its value. The payout is emptied and destroyed after merging.

The merchant's hand contact is always selectable: Grip starts conversation while idle. When a valid offer exists, it displays a small "Grip to agree" cue and Grip calls `VendorCounter.TryCommit`; a forgiving controller-grip proximity check provides a fallback if XRI selection misses. The commit rechecks every offered object, final prices, purse position, and both balances. It creates a payout pouch before any mutation, changes goods and net currency once, and rolls back on an exception. Sold goods become merchant owned and their grab component is disabled. Bought goods remain on the counter for pickup.

Do not mark the merchant feature complete until those checks pass.

# Scavenger enemy NPC

Drag `Generated/Scavenger_JKR88_Ammo.prefab` (the complete JKR-88 with its R4 cell), `Generated/Scavenger_JKR88.prefab`, or `Generated/Scavenger_PKO.prefab` into a scene with a baked NavMesh. `Generated/ScavengerDemo.unity` is a separate cover-combat test scene. The setup does not edit your gameplay scenes. The ammo variant is generated only when `Assets/MyAssets/prefabs/weapons/JKR-88 1.prefab` exists.

`Generated/ScavengerRestartSign.prefab` is a shootable scene reset sign, already placed near the Junker in `ScavengerDemo.unity`. A laser impact reloads its scene, restoring the enemies and other scene objects. Use **Tools > Scavenger NPC > Place restart sign in active scene** to add one to another scene at the Scene view pivot. For a player build, include that scene in Build Settings.

## Scene setup

- The NPC automatically follows the active Junker rig's HMD and uses its root for sight checks, even if an older demo instance still references the stationary test target. For other player rigs, assign **Target** to the moving HMD/camera and **Target Root** to the rig, or leave Target empty to use `Camera.main`.
- Add `ScavengerPlayerHealth` to the player root that contains the CharacterController, or use another `IDamageable` implementation there. The Tools menu can add this component to the selected rig. Connect its damage/death UnityEvents to your HUD and respawn flow; it does not move or disable VR tracking.
- Set **Idle Action** to Standing, Casual, Patrol, or Sitting. Casual wanders around its spawn point. Patrol follows **Patrol Points**. For sitting, assign **Seat** to a floor-level marker facing away from the chair back; the procedural pose lowers the pelvis approximately 43 cm. Use a chair with a seat around 45 cm high. Standing/sitting/aiming are procedural poses, not new authored animation clips.
- Use **Tools > Scavenger NPC > Create cover and peek markers**. Place the root on the NavMesh behind a solid wall. Add entries to **Peek Points**: `Side` points sit on the NavMesh beyond a wall edge; `Over` points remain at the cover root and specify the standing sight height above a low wall. The enemy tries each valid point in turn, alternating sides when both can see the player. It crouches while hiding if **Crouch When Hiding** is enabled, rises in place for an `Over` peek, shoots, then crouches again. Set **Crouched Sight Height** below the wall top and each `Over` point's **Sight Height** above it. The NPC requires hidden sight to be blocked and at least one usable peek marker; it checks the actual firing lane before each shot and reserves cover so enemies do not share it.
- Tune detection range/FOV, movement speeds, memory, hide/peek durations and their independent time variation, health, burst timing, spread, and damage in the Inspector. Sight and weapon masks must include walls and player colliders. Physics queries ignore triggers and the NPC's own colliders. Peek entries with an old serialized sight height of zero use a 1.45 m standing sight height.
- **Rush Attack Range** lets a hiding NPC abandon cover and fire immediately when the player reaches a clear standing firing lane; it briefly avoids selecting cover again. Each `ScavengerWeapon` has **Aim Precision** (0–1) and **Spread Degrees**. These affect both the simple gun and the ammo pistol, so NPC shots scatter around the player's HMD instead of always converging on it.

The NPC detects the player, runs to valid nearby cover, waits, peeks, fires a burst, and retreats. Without valid/reachable cover it attacks from its current position. Losing sight makes it search the last known location; after the memory timeout it resumes its idle action. Taking damage alerts it. A missing NavMesh logs a warning and prevents movement without disabling stationary combat. Every generated variant with a configured ammo belt fires through `JunkerPistol`, consumes real cell charge, and reloads from its three spares. Once the belt is empty, it cannot fire until given another cell.

The `Junker` prefab now includes `ScavengerPlayerHealth` on its root so hits on child colliders reach the player. Set **Max Health** there. Hits flash dark-red edges in the headset; the edge effect grows as health falls. At zero health the component raises **On Death** and disables artificial move/jump input while preserving head tracking. **Restore()** returns health and locomotion. Edge and flash opacity and flash duration are Inspector controls. The old inactive test target retains its separate health component for non-VR testing.

The headset blood effect also exposes **Screen Scale**, **Edge Start**, **Edge Softness**, **Drip Count**, **Drip Length**, **Drip Width**, and **Blood Color**. Smaller Screen Scale and Edge Start values bring the blood farther into view. The blood texture updates while tuning these fields in Play mode.

All generated scavengers carry three visible R4 cells at editable **Slot Offsets** on `ScavengerAmmoBelt`. The ammo pistol ejects an empty cell and inserts the next charged waist cell after **Reload Seconds** on `ScavengerWeapon`; it stops once the belt is empty. On death the gun drops with physics and its XR grab enabled, and unused cells fall as grabbable loot. The two visual-only legacy guns receive a physical socket, loaded cell, and usable player pistol component at runtime, so they follow the same ammo and loot flow. Rebuilding generated prefabs includes the belt when the R4 prefab is present.

Nonlethal hits briefly bend and twist the upper chest while the normal movement and aiming continue. Tune **Hit Duration**, **Hit Bend Degrees**, and **Hit Twist Degrees** on `ScavengerPose`. Fatal hits skip the flinch and activate the ragdoll immediately. There is no separate hit animation clip in the source asset, so this reaction is procedural and also works on already placed scavengers.

The gun is a child of the right-hand bone, so it stays attached while previewing animation in Edit mode and when the pose component is disabled. The kinematic ragdoll bodies have interpolation off while animation controls the skeleton; interpolation starts only when death activates physics. If a scene instance looks separated, use **Tools > Scavenger NPC > Diagnose visual rig** to write its bone, mesh, scale, and weapon positions to `Library/Scavenger-visual.txt`.

To align the replacement pistol, select a scavenger scene instance and choose **Tools > Scavenger NPC > Preview selected scavenger aim**. In its `ScavengerPose` Inspector, adjust **Gun Grip Position** and **Gun Grip Euler** while outside Play mode; the gun follows these hand-local offsets immediately. **Preview Aim Point Local** controls what the arms aim toward. Turn off **Preview Aim In Editor** when finished. Apply the component overrides to your prefab to reuse the grip. `JunkerEquipmentItem`'s grip offsets are for a VR player's grab point and do not position the NPC's gun.

Player `JunkerPistol` shots now route to the existing `IDamageable` contract, retaining `JunkerTarget` fallback. Thus PKO and JKR-88 can kill enemies. On death, navigation and animation stop, cover is released, and thirteen constrained rigid bodies take over. The actual pistol detaches from the hand and becomes grabbable; spare cells ignore their own corpse when they fall so overlapping waist colliders cannot launch the ragdoll. Ragdoll depenetration speed and damping are Inspector controls. Corpses persist until your game removes them.

## Terrain treadmill

The scavenger's `NavMeshAgent` needs a live NavMesh at the current terrain height. `PlanetTerrainTreadmill` moves/reuses Terrain tiles and supplies colliders, but it does not create or update NavMesh data. A one-time bake in the demo scene will not follow recycled tiles. Bake or stream matching navigation data for each active logical tile (including walkable cover surroundings), and move/remove that data when `CenterTileChanged` fires or the floating origin shifts. Spawn the NPC and its patrol and cover markers on the active tile's NavMesh; when that tile leaves the visible window, despawn or relocate its NPCs and markers. Cover walls need colliders that block sight, and cover roots and side-peek markers need reachable NavMesh positions. The current AI uses world `Vector3.up` and assumes flat Y-up gravity; curved/spherical gravity needs additional movement and sight changes.

## Rig repair and assets

The source rig has no individual finger/thumb joints. Exactly 201 vertices on the right thumb had weights assigned to `mixamorig:RightUpLeg`; those weights are transferred to `mixamorig:RightHand`, merged, and checked for normalization in all five GLBs. Original source files remain untouched. The prefabs use repaired copies under `Models`.

Reproduce the repair with `python tools/repair_scavenger_skin.py` (NumPy required). Rebuild generated assets using **Tools > Scavenger NPC > Build enemy prefabs and test scene**. This regenerates the prefabs and demo; keep custom variants elsewhere. **Validate generated prefabs** checks bone references, clip bindings, and ragdoll setup.

The supplied walk/run clips animate the Generic skeleton; horizontal root translation is removed so the NavMeshAgent owns locomotion. Arm IK positions both hands for aiming. Grip offsets and hand rotation fields on `ScavengerPose` are exposed for artistic adjustment; the simplified source hands have no independently movable fingers.

The navigation and procedural seating currently assume a conventional Unity Y-up surface. They are not wired into the project's planet/gravity-frame transitions.

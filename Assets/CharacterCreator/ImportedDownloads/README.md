# Downloaded character wardrobe

SourceMeshes/ contains 25 GLBs selected from the numbered textured_mesh files in Downloads. Numbered files 8–17 were ship parts and were excluded. BeardSource/ contains five MakeHuman beard and moustache OBJ assets from bodyparts05_cc0.zip; the archive identifies them as CC0 and includes author and source metadata in packs/bodyparts05.json.

Items/ contains 32 fitted preview prefabs and AppearanceItemDefinition assets. Six hairstyles and five facial hair pieces are available through CosmeticCatalog.asset. Twenty-one garments and head accessories are available to the clothing matcher. Each item records its attachment bone, style family, and fit note. The headwear collection has two original spiked helmets, two bronze/black visual variants, a horned headpiece, wrapped headgear, and two desert hoods.

Open CharacterCreatorMorphPOC.unity and press Play. Use Next hair, Next beard, and Match outfit in the creator panel. In the character Inspector, Randomize all clothing selects a coherent desert, scavenger, or raider family. Individual clothing slots can still be assigned manually. The importer can be rerun with Tools > Character Creator > Import Downloaded Wardrobe; Validate Downloaded Wardrobe checks all item bounds and catalog entries.

The six hairstyles now use the exact local transforms authored on the six CombinedStarvingBandits in `Assets/Scenes/Fittings.unity`. Their values are built into `DownloadedWardrobeImporter`, so rerunning the wardrobe import preserves the fit. The `CharacterAppearanceController` Inspector offers a **Hair Type** dropdown listing the actual catalog items by name. `NPCCharacterAppearance` no longer changes the prefab's Inspector appearance in `Awake`; archetype generation and preset application happen only when explicitly requested through its Inspector buttons or API. The duel scene builder requests generation while it creates the scene, so those authored fighters also keep the same appearance in Play Mode.

The downloaded GLBs and beard OBJs have no CC_Base skin weights or body morphs. **Tools > Character Creator > Bake Fitted Wardrobe Rig** generates `RiggedItems/` with CC_Base weights and four body-size morphs for eleven body-worn items: the armored torso, scavenger vest, three skirts, wrapped boots, both shoulder harnesses, the scavenger outfit, and both gloves. Each glove item now includes both hands. The skirt prefabs include fitted thigh and shin plates. The boot prefab mirrors the source wrap, turns both wraps upright, blends their upper sections to the calf, and adds fitted foot shells. The eleven item definitions used by the matcher point to these skinned prefabs after baking. Headwear remains rigid to the head bone and needs visual clipping review at face shape extremes. The source GLB license was not present in Downloads; confirm usage rights before distribution.

The October fit pass adjusts hair toward the rear of the head and increases its front-to-back depth. It also sizes skirts, the vest, shoulder pieces, the armored torso, the full outfit, and the boot using their actual shapes rather than one shared width assumption. See [CLOTHING_RIGGING_PLAN.md](CLOTHING_RIGGING_PLAN.md) for the next steps toward body-size and pose-aware clothing.

Temporary render previews are in Temp/CharacterCreatorDownloadsPreview and are not part of the Unity Assets import.
